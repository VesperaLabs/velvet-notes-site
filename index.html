Velvet Notes

A fast, local-first Android notes workspace crafted for OLED displays — featuring client-side AES-256-GCM vault encryption and private Google Drive sync.

Privacy Policy Vespera Labs on GitHub

Local-First Architecture

Every note, checklist, and folder lives directly on your device for instant offline access with zero lock-in.

AES-256-GCM Vault

Locked notes are encrypted client-side using a key derived from your PIN or passphrase before ever leaving your phone.

Private Drive Sync

Optional sync uses Google Drive's isolated hidden App Data folder — Velvet Notes cannot read your personal Drive files.

Zero Tracking

No ads, no analytics SDKs, and no external developer servers storing your personal notes.
